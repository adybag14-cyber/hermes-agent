Before you join

Does joining the group complete my Play signup?

No. Group membership controls eligibility. Google Play’s own opt-in step is separate, and only becomes useful when the closed release is available. This page does not claim that you have completed that step.

Which Google account should I use?

Use the account selected in the Play Store on your Android device. If you have several Google accounts, check which one is active on both the group page and the Play testing page.

If you already joined the app’s internal test, leave that test before opting in to the closed test. Internal testing is not the same as the closed Alpha.

What is expected of testers?

Try the app in ordinary use and report reproducible problems. Include the app version, Android version, steps to reproduce, and what you expected. Do not include API keys, passwords or private conversations.

Joining this group alone does not start Google Play’s qualifying test period. Please keep feedback genuine; creating accounts or joining a list is not a substitute for testing.

What information does this page collect?

This page has no email form, account database, analytics script or embedded sign-in widget. Your membership and test choices are handled on Google’s own pages. The group owners and managers can see your membership and email address.

GitHub processes requests to host this page, and Google processes group membership and Play opt-in under their own policies. Read our privacy policy for details. You can leave the Google Group and opt out of the Play test using Google’s controls.
